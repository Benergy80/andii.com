The stone is never only a pedestal.

Every material carries its own energy, and we work with it as a participant: to bring balance and intention to the spaces we inhabit.

1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. Selenite. Clarity, cleansing. Gypsum crystal that carries light through its body. In Dolmen, Selene.
  2. Calcite. Calm, amplification. Calcite splits a single ray of light in two. In Zenith, Mirage.
  3. Obsidian. Protection, grounding. Volcanic glass, polished into some of the first mirrors. In Scry.
  4. Pyrite. Abundance, will. Named for fire: strike it and it sparks. In Dyad in Pyrite, The Weight of Glimmer, Ore I, Ore II.
  5. Sapphire. Wisdom, focus. Second only to diamond in hardness. In Reflecting Pool.
  6. Quartz. Intention, harmony. It resonates at a fixed frequency; quartz keeps time in our clocks. In DropShine I–III, Drips.
